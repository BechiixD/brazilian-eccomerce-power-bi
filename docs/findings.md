# Findings and Recommendations

*Period: Jan–Aug 2018, delivered orders only. Values marked ~ are read from the charts.*

## At a glance
| Revenue | Orders | AOV | Avg Review | Late deliveries | Repeat customers |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $7.22M | 53K | $137 | 4.14 / 5 | 8% | 3.3% (all time) |

## 1. The business is growing fast, and growth is steady
- Revenue grew **+141%** compared with the same months of 2017 (Jan–Aug).
- In 2018, monthly revenue was stable at **$0.83M–0.98M**. May was the strongest month and February the weakest.
- **November 2017** was the highest month in the dataset, most likely because of Black Friday. The data covers only one November, so this shows a peak but does not yet prove a seasonal pattern.

**Recommendation:** Plan stock, logistics and campaigns ahead of November, and confirm the pattern with 2018 data.

## 2. Revenue is concentrated in São Paulo and a few categories
- **São Paulo** generates **~$2.9M (~40%)** of revenue, about 3× the next states, RJ and MG (~$0.85M each).
- The top categories are **health_beauty**, **watches_gifts** and **bed_bath_table**.
- The North and Northeast states bring little revenue and have the worst delivery (see 3).

**Recommendation:** Protect SP, the core market, with fast delivery. Treat the Northeast as a growth opportunity that first needs better logistics.

## 3. Late deliveries destroy customer satisfaction
- On-time orders average **~4.3 stars**. Late orders average **~2.3**, about **2 points lower**.
- The longer the delay, the lower the score: early ~4.3, 1-3 days late ~3.3, 4-7 days late ~2.0, +7 days late ~1.7.
- Late rates are highest in **AL (~22%)**, **MA (~20%)** and **CE (~19%)**, against an 8% average.

**Recommendation:** Delivery is the strongest lever for reviews. Use more realistic delivery estimates for the Northeast, monitor carriers by state, and contact customers proactively when an order will be late.

## 4. Almost no customers come back
- **96.7%** of customers bought only once in their whole history. Only **3.3%** ever bought again.
- Counting only orders placed within Jan–Aug 2018, the repeat rate is just **2.1%**.
- About **6K new customers** arrive every month, against only ~100–200 returning ones.

**Recommendation:** Growth depends entirely on acquisition, which is expensive. Launch retention actions (post-purchase emails, second-purchase coupons, loyalty) and track the repeat rate as a core KPI. Fixing late deliveries (point 3) should also help retention.

## 5. Low dependency on individual sellers
- The **top 10 sellers** make up only **13%** of revenue. The largest single seller accounts for **1.9%**.
- The Pareto curve rises slowly, so revenue is spread across many sellers.

**Recommendation:** Losing any one seller is a low risk. Watch top sellers with weak reviews (one top-10 seller averages **3.42**), because their volume can hurt the marketplace's reputation.

## Limitations
- Data runs only to Aug 2018, so seasonality cannot be confirmed.
- Revenue excludes freight, and there is no cost or margin data.
- Reviews are joined at the order level, so an order with several items counts its review once per order.
